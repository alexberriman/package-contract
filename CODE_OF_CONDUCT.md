# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Put the health of the community ahead of individual advantage.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, trolling,
and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through the
contact method listed in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed
promptly, fairly, and with respect for the reporter's privacy.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that violate this policy. Temporary or permanent bans may
be applied when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project.
